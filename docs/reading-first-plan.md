# Reading first: iteration plan

The product should preserve a research trail quietly, then reconstruct the specific ideas, mechanisms and disagreements in that trail. Browsing volume is not a learning goal.

Keep the supplied identity: lilac paper (`--paper`, #F4F3F8), white surface, plum ink, violet selection and the dark burrow. Amber remains a capture/highlight signal. Geologica is the UI; Literata means quoted source words. No new palette or decorative animation.

The map becomes a reading guide: a larger central idea with a few takeaways, major branches and smaller details. Radial is the default; chronological depth remains an alternate view. Graph placement is navigation, not an evidence claim. Only saved relationships draw edges. The inspector leads with explanation, mechanism/example and limitation; short exact evidence is expandable rather than dominating the panel.

Reading mode closes the side panel and leaves a small, static rabbit control on included research pages, with pause and reopen controls. No counters competing with the article. Automatic selection capture is an explicit per-session opt-in, debounced, excluded from editable fields, and still checked by the worker against tab/session/pause boundaries.

Practice opens on an objectively checked 2×2 quiz. Distractors target plausible misconceptions, with reasons for each option from Go. Matching provides a second structured activity. Existing open-response and flashcards remain optional. No local string comparison impersonates AI assessment.

Strengthen Go’s saved output with a source-grounded overview, hierarchy, takeaways, concrete examples, limitations and exact supporting excerpts. Optional fields keep older maps readable. Explicit rebuild is allowed for already-processed sources without inventing captures or silently regenerating content. Go still requires a prompt handoff and approved save.

Defer notifications, scheduled reviews, cross-session maps, memory-strength claims and the rest of the activity catalogue until this loop is demonstrably useful.

Verification: reproduce disclosure collapse, preserve it across real background refresh, exercise automatic selection opt-in/off/paused/editable exclusions and compact indicator controls; validate hierarchy/evidence/matching payloads, legacy compatibility and rebuild version protection; test radial/trail keyboard navigation, quiet edges, quiz/matching grading and persistence; retain capture/restart/delete regressions and 320px/dark/reduced-motion checks. Synthetic tests do not establish quality of real Go generation; inspect a fresh real run separately.
