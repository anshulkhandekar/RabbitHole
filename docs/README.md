## Historical evidence

Stage 1–3, reading-first and redesign documents retain the decisions and observations at their dates. Their tool counts, layouts, release numbers and open issues do not override the current guides. The October 28 [release evaluation](release-evaluation-2026-10-28.md) is evidence of that build, not final live sign-off. Saved testing prompts reproduce earlier audits and should be adapted to the current checklist.

Private retest reports and generated screenshots live under ignored `test-results/`; they may not exist in a fresh clone. Repository documentation must stand on its own. No personal research database, credentials or account screenshots are required to understand or build the project.
